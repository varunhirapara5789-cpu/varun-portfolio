01 / About

The story so far

Connecting the lab
with production.

I'm a Chemical & Bioprocess Engineer with an M.Sc. from Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH). My experience spans microbial research, bioprocess development and industrial polymer production.

At EveryCarbon, I help run development experiments, operate bioreactors, optimize membrane filtration and support scale-up from 2 L laboratory systems to 100 L pilot scale. My work includes aseptic sampling, sensor calibration, HPLC/GC analysis, process troubleshooting and GMP-oriented documentation.

Before that, I worked with polymerisation and extrusion processes at Gujarat Fluorochemicals and carried out research in microbiology and carbon materials. I also use Python to model experimental processes.
